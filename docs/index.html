How it works

Booking an NCT test through ncts.ie often means checking back day after day waiting for a slot to open at a centre near you. This tool does that checking for you: every hour, it walks the same booking flow you would and reads the real availability at every test centre in the country.

Sign up with your email, tell it which centres work for you and how soon you need a test, and confirm your email address. From then on you'll only hear from it when there's actually something to book — no noise, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is this run by the NCTS? No — this is an independent, unofficial tool that reads the same publicly available booking calendar you'd see on ncts.ie. It doesn't book anything on your behalf; it just tells you when to go book it yourself.

Is my email shared with anyone? No — it's used only to send you availability alerts for the centres you chose.
