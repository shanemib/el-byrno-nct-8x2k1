Frequently asked questions

Is this run by the NCTS?

No — this is an independent, unofficial tool that reads the same publicly available booking calendar you'd see on ncts.ie. It doesn't book anything on your behalf; it just tells you when to go book it yourself.

Is my email shared with anyone?

No — it's used only to send you availability alerts for the centres you chose.

How often does it check?

Every hour, automatically. You don't need to keep a tab open or do anything after you confirm your email.

Will I get spammed with emails?

No — you're only emailed when a new slot matching your centres and time window appears, and never emailed twice about the same one.
