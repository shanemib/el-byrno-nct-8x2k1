Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who runs this

NCT Appointment Alerts is an independent, unofficial project — it is not run by, or affiliated with, the NCTS or Applus. It's a single-person side project. Questions or requests about your data can be raised via the project's GitHub repository.

What we collect

When you sign up: your email address (required), the test centres and time window you choose, and a WhatsApp number (optional, only collected ahead of a future paid feature — it is not used for anything today). That's the complete list. We don't collect your name, vehicle registration, payment details, or location.

Why we collect it

Solely to run the alert service: to send you a one-time confirmation email, to match your chosen centres/window against live availability, and to email (or in future, WhatsApp) you when a match is found. Nothing here is used for advertising targeting, profiling, or sold or shared with anyone for marketing purposes.

Who else sees it

Your data passes through a small number of service providers that make the site work, each acting strictly as a processor on our behalf: Supabase (hosts the database), Resend (delivers alert and confirmation emails), Twilio (will deliver WhatsApp messages, only for subscribers who opt into the future paid plan), and GitHub Pages (hosts this website). None of them are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Cookies and ads

This site does not currently run any analytics or advertising, and sets no tracking cookies. If Google AdSense is enabled here in future, Google may set cookies to serve and measure ads — if that happens, this page will be updated, and you can read Google's own policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How long we keep it

If you sign up but never confirm your email, that unconfirmed entry is simply overwritten if you sign up again, and isn't actively used for anything in the meantime. Once you unsubscribe (a link is included in every alert email), your record is deleted outright — not just marked inactive.

Your choices

You can update your centres, time window, or WhatsApp number at any time using the "manage your alerts" link in your emails, or remove yourself completely using the unsubscribe link — no account, login, or email back-and-forth required either way.
